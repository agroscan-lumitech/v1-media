THE SHORT VERSION

An IoT sensor in the soil. An AI mind behind the screen. A farmer back in control.

Luminary AgroScan is an integrated agricultural technology ecosystem that combines Internet of Things (IoT) hardware, Artificial Intelligence, and cloud-based software to improve crop productivity, soil analysis accuracy and general awareness of modern farming — built to promote a self-reliant, Aatmanirbhar farming future.

01

Sense

AgroSoilManager and AgroClimateManager read pH, moisture, NPK, temperature, light, rainfall and humidity directly from the field — solar-powered, low-cost and rugged.

Understand

WHY AGROSCAN EXISTS

Surveys across Dumri and Muzaffarpur found something the team couldn't ignore.

Over 70% of local farmers had never conducted a soil test — not out of disinterest, but because affordable tools, scientific knowledge and digital access simply weren't within reach. Weather dependence, guesswork-driven crop selection, and a widening gap between agricultural potential and practical resources were the norm, not the exception.

That gap between ground reality and digital innovation is exactly what Team AgroScan set out to bridge.
