AgroClimateManager

Smart climate monitoring for sustainable farming — solar-powered, self-sustaining, built for harsh agricultural environments.

WHY CLIMATE MONITORING MATTERS

Farmers depend on unpredictable rainfall. That's the risk this device removes.

Modern agriculture operates under increasing environmental pressure. Farmers depend heavily on unpredictable rainfall and fluctuating weather, making precise environmental data essential for risk mitigation — mitigating reliance on unpredictable weather, optimising water use, and maximising yield potential.

Data Scarcity

Lack of hyper-local, real-time weather data for immediate field-level decisions.

Unpredictable Rainfall

Difficulty forecasting localised heavy rain or prolonged dry spells, leading to poor scheduling.

Resource Wastage

Manual processes often over-irrigate or under-water, depleting resources and raising costs.

Crop Vulnerability

Higher risk of crop loss from drought or unexpected heavy-rain damage.

THE SOLUTION

A rugged, self-powered IoT device, built to be affordable.

AgroClimateManager is a modular, low-cost, solar-assisted IoT device providing accurate, localised climate data essential for modern irrigation management and farm planning — using hybrid solar and wind energy for continuous, off-grid operation.

ComponentFunction
Rain Detection RodsDetects the onset of rain and triggers immediate alerts.
Mini Windmill GeneratorMeasures wind speed and provides supplementary power.
Temperature & Humidity SensorMonitors local air temperature and humidity.
Buzzer & Battery UnitSends audible alerts to farmers and stores energy for night use.
Solar PanelProvides continuous renewable energy for autonomous operation.

The working principle: data to action

The system operates autonomously, converting environmental input into actionable information. When rain is detected, the detection rods close a circuit; controller logic verifies the input and processes the data; the result reaches the farmer as a data log and an immediate alert.

Connectivity & impact

Sends data to the AgroScan App via LoRa or BLE. Alerts appear on the app dashboard and the on-site buzzer activates. Data is stored in the AgroScan cloud and analysed for weather trends.

  • Prevents over-irrigation during unexpected rainfall.
  • Improves irrigation timing, saving water and power.
  • Enhances climate resilience by predicting local weather changes.
V2.0 & BEYOND

AgroClimateManager v2.0

Adds a UV sensor, barometric pressure module, and AI-based rainfall prediction.

Shared platform upgrades

Edge AI integration, modular swappable sensor heads, and ~30% extended battery life via adaptive solar charging.
