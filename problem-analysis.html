Home / About / The Problem We Solve

The problem was never a lack of effort. It was a lack of information.

A full breakdown of the situation, causes, effects and future challenges facing Indian farmers today — and exactly how AgroScan responds to each one.

1 · CURRENT SITUATION

Five pressures on every farmer's margin.

Soil & crop management

Poor access to soil testing leads to inefficient fertilization and irrigation.

Weather dependence

Farmers lack real-time rainfall, temperature and humidity data, leading to crop losses.

Limited agricultural knowledge

Low literacy and minimal digital access make modern practices, pest control and schemes hard to adopt.

Market & pricing issues

Lack of access to fair market prices and local vendors reduces income.

Manual, labor-intensive work

Traditional farming is physically demanding, discouraging youth from agriculture.

2 · ROOT CAUSES

Why the situation persists

  • High cost or unavailability of monitoring tools for soil, water and weather.
  • Knowledge gaps from limited training, government programs and extension services.
  • Inefficient resource management — overuse of fertilizers, pesticides and water.
  • Technological barriers: language issues, low digital literacy, lack of devices.
  • Fragmented government support, making schemes and subsidies hard to access.
3 · EFFECTS

What it costs farmers

  • Low productivity from poor soil and irrigation management.
  • Economic stress — inefficient farming and low profits leading to debt.
  • Declining interest in agriculture among younger generations.
  • Environmental impact from fertilizer and water overuse.
  • Food security concerns from post-harvest losses.
4 · ADDITIONAL PROBLEMS AGROSCAN ADDRESSES

Post-harvest losses — poor storage and transport cause crop wastage.

Limited financial access — small farmers struggle to get credit for tools or recovery.

Water scarcity — many farms rely solely on monsoon rain.

Climate extremes — heatwaves, floods and unseasonal rain threaten crops.

Depleting resources — overuse of soil, water and fertilizer reduces long-term output.

Infrastructure gaps — poor roads, storage and market access hurt profitability.

5 · FUTURE CHALLENGES

What's coming, if nothing changes

A shrinking farmer population as urban migration continues, a rising need for automation to meet future food demand, and an urgent case for sustainability and resource efficiency before further degradation sets in.

6 · HOW AGROSCAN RESPONDS

Hardware for sensing. Software for guidance. Awareness for change.

AgroSoilManager and AgroClimateManager provide real-time monitoring; the User App and Developer Application integrate AI-based crop guidance, AgroPedia knowledge, the AgroShop marketplace, and real-time alerts — with multilingual support and scheme guidance built in from day one.
