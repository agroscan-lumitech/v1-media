Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about AgroScan — what it is, what it costs, and how it works.

What is Project AgroScan?
Project AgroScan is a free, student-built AI and IoT agricultural technology ecosystem that gives farmers real-time soil, climate and crop guidance through low-cost hardware sensors, a farmer-facing app, and an AI engine called AgroAI. It was founded by Abhinav Ranjan under the parent organisation Luminary Technicals.
Does AgroScan cost anything to use?
No. AgroScan is completely free of cost for every farmer, always. The project's stated goal is to support small farmers without ever charging users.
What hardware does AgroScan use?
Two solar-powered IoT devices: AgroSoilManager, which measures soil pH, moisture, temperature and light; and AgroClimateManager, which tracks rainfall, wind, temperature and humidity.
Does AgroScan work without internet?
Yes. The app is designed with Offline & Sync Mode — it remains fully functional without internet access and automatically synchronises data once a connection returns, which matters for farmers in low-connectivity areas.
Who built AgroScan?
A student team — Abhinav Ranjan (Founder & Project Director), Sudhanshu Kumar (Frontend), Abhiraj Singh (Backend & DevEnd), and Naitik Jha (Hardware) — mentored by coordinators Mr. Dhananjay, Mr. Awadhesh and Mr. Anand, under Luminary Technicals.
Where was AgroScan tested?
At Shemford Futuristic School's Dumri and Chakkar branches in Muzaffarpur, Bihar, across two public exhibitions, plus direct village surveys in Dumri that shaped the original concept.
What is AgroAI?
AgroAI is AgroScan's AI engine, combining a Crop Recommender Model, a Fertilizer Optimization Model, and a Weather Intelligence Model — plus a hybrid local rule-engine so recommendations still work offline.
How accurate is AgroScan's soil testing?
Field testing measured 91% soil test accuracy against lab-verified samples, alongside a 95% user satisfaction rating for ease of use.
Is my farm data private?
AgroScan uses a seven-layer security framework including HTTPS, AES-256 encryption, token-based access and role-based permissions. See the full Security Framework page for details, including what's still being hardened.
How can I try AgroScan?
Open the live user app at agroscan-org.netlify.app, or download the Android app via the QR code shared in the project's promotional materials.
Who is the Project Director?
Ar. Abhinav Ranjan — Founder and Project Director of Project AgroScan, and founder of Luminary Technicals. See his dedicated profile page for more, including his portfolio.
What's next for AgroScan?
v1.3 will add AgroCloud storage and predictive crop analytics; hardware v2.0 adds new sensors to both devices; longer-term plans include AgroWaterManager, AgroPestMonitor and AgroDroneLink. See the full Future Plans page.
