Home / Journey / Public Review History

Four visits. Four rounds of honest feedback.

The complete, dated visit history behind AgroScan — objectives, activities and outcomes for each stop.

Pre-Foundation Visit

Idea Formation

📍 Location: Dumri Local Village, Muzaffarpur, Bihar

Objective: Identify real farmer problems to form the idea of AgroScan.

Activities: Discussions with local farmers and small-scale agricultural workers; preliminary observation of soil, irrigation and crop management; collection of informal insights on productivity, schemes and cost constraints.

Outcome: Formulated the core concept of AgroScan, including hardware and software solutions, and defined the initial feature set for v1.0.

Foundation Visit (v1.0)

Idea & Logic

📍 Location: Online surveys, live farm visits, public experience

Objective: Conduct structured research to validate ideas and understand detailed farmer problems.

Activities: Interviews with farmers on daily challenges; soil checks and farm inspections; observation of irrigation practices, yields and resource constraints.

Outcome: Research-driven foundation for the AgroScan prototype v1.0.

Public Awareness Visit (v1.1)

Art Exhibition

📍 Location: Shemford Futuristic School, Dumri, Muzaffarpur

Objective: Test v1.0 publicly and collect feedback for v1.1.

Activities: Demonstrations, Q&A, and suggestion collection.

Outcome: Improvements suggested for multi-language support, user suggestions, and AgroShop integration.

Public Review Visit (v1.2)

Science Exhibition

📍 Location: Shemford Futuristic School, Chakkar Branch, Muzaffarpur

Objective: Gather user reviews and ideas for future updates.

Activities: Demonstrations and live interaction with users; feedback focused on developer interaction and UX enhancements.

Outcome: Led to v1.2 improvements with live developer chat and better accessibility.
