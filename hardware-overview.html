Home / Hardware

Two devices. Solar-powered. Built for a real field, not a lab bench.

AgroClimateManager and AgroSoilManager are the physical core of AgroScan — bridging environmental conditions and digital intelligence.

The AgroScan Hardware System is the physical core of the ecosystem. Each device is designed for field durability, accuracy and ease of use, connecting directly with the AgroScan Backend and RepoEnd to send real-time data and receive firmware or system updates.

AgroClimateManager

A smart, solar-powered IoT device that continuously monitors microclimatic conditions — helping farmers predict rainfall, manage irrigation schedules, and react quickly to environmental change.

Full device page →

AgroSoilManager

A portable, immersion-rod-type IoT device for soil health analysis — measuring temperature, pH, moisture and light intensity to guide crop and fertilizer decisions.

Full device page →
INTEGRATION & COMMUNICATION

One protocol, both devices.

Wireless Mode

BLE/LoRa → AgroScan Backend → RepoEnd archive.

Power Mode

Solar-assisted with battery backup for 24/7 operation.

Update Mode

OTA firmware updates triggered via the RepoEnd API.
